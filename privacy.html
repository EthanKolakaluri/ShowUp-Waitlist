Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to hold your spot, take your payment and plan hangouts.
  • Stripe handles your card. We never see your full card number.
  • We don't sell your personal information or use it for ads.
  • In the app, location is only used to confirm check-ins for hangouts you accept, around the start time.
  • You can ask us to see, fix or delete your information anytime.

1. Who we are

[legal business name] (“ShowUp”, “we”) runs ShowUp. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. It covers our website and the founding member waitlist. Before the app launches, we'll update it to cover the app and let you know what changed.

2. What we collect

When you reserve a spot

  • Your first name, email address, city, age range and the interests you pick.
  • Your confirmation that you're 18 or older.

When you pay

Stripe collects your card details and billing information directly. We receive the payment result, the amount, your name and email, and the billing country or postal code Stripe asks for. We never see or store your full card number.

Automatically

  • Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Like most websites, these services receive your IP address and basic browser details when you load a page. We don't use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies.
  • The site saves your first name, city and reservation reference in your browser's local storage, so the confirmation page can greet you. You can clear it anytime in your browser settings.

When you contact us

Whatever you choose to include in your message.

3. When the app launches

To run ShowUp, the app will also use:

  • Your match quiz answers, to match you with a crew.
  • Your location, only to confirm check-ins: only for hangouts you've accepted, only around the start time, and only with your permission. We don't track your location in the background.
  • Which hangouts you attend and the credits you earn.

We'll describe these in detail in an updated policy before launch.

4. How we use your information

  • To hold your founding spot and process your payment and any refund.
  • To send you confirmations, receipts, launch updates and your match quiz.
  • To decide which cities to launch in, and when.
  • To keep ShowUp safe, prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.

5. Who we share it with

Only the service providers that help us run ShowUp, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Stripe, to process payments and refunds.
  • Google, which stores our waitlist records (Google Sheets) and sends our emails (Gmail).
  • GitHub, which hosts our website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case we'll tell you first.

6. Emails

We email you about your reservation, payment and any refund, and send updates about ShowUp's launch. If you don't want launch updates, reply “unsubscribe” and we'll stop. We'll still send messages about your payment or account.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're on the waitlist or a member. If you ask us to delete it, we will, except for payment and refund records we have to keep for tax and accounting.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we have about you and send you a copy;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • stop sending you launch updates.

Email [contact email]. We'll respond within 30 days and won't treat you differently for asking.

Depending on where you live, for example California, you may have more rights under local law, and you can have an authorized agent make a request for you. We don't sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

9. Security

We use trusted providers and limit access to your information to the people who need it to run ShowUp. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Age limit

ShowUp is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has signed up, email us and we'll delete their information.

11. Where your information is stored

Our providers store information in the United States.

12. Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll email you before it takes effect.
