What people ask before they join

When am I charged?

Once, $9.99, when you reserve. That also pays for your first month after launch. Your next charge is at the start of month two, at the same $9.99 founding price.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Before launch you can get a full refund anytime, with one click from your confirmation email. After launch, the VibeSwift Guarantee covers your first month. See the refund policy.

Do I pay for the hangouts themselves?

Your plan covers matching and planning. At venues you pay your own way, within a budget you set. Free options like hikes, parks and game nights are always on the table.

I'm under 21. Does that matter?

If anyone in a crew is under 21, the planner only picks all-ages spots.

What if my crew doesn't click?

Re-match anytime on Crew or Social Club. The VibeSwift Guarantee covers your first month.

Is my location tracked?

Only to verify check-ins, only for hangouts you've accepted, and only during the start window.

Is this a dating app?

No. VibeSwift is for friendships only.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, anytime. Your credits come with you.
