Refund Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

The short version

  • Before launch: a full refund anytime, for any reason.
  • It takes one click from your confirmation email.
  • After launch: the VibeSwift Guarantee covers your first month.
  • Monthly membership: cancel anytime and you won't be charged again.

1. Before VibeSwift launches

You can get a full refund of your $9.99 reservation anytime before launch, for any reason.

How to get a refund

  • Click Refund my reservation in your confirmation email, or the refund link on the page you saw after checkout, then confirm. It goes through right away.
  • Or email us at [contact email] from the address you reserved with.

The money goes back to the card you paid with. It usually shows up in 5–10 business days, depending on your bank. We'll email you when it's on its way.

A refund releases your founding spot and your founding price. If you change your mind later, you're welcome to reserve again while founding spots last. You'll get a new spot number.

2. If we don't launch

If we decide not to launch VibeSwift, we'll refund every reservation in full. You won't need to ask.

3. After launch: the VibeSwift Guarantee

Your reservation pays for your first month after launch.

Go to 3 hangouts in your first 30 days. If you haven't met at least one person you want to see again, email us and we'll refund your first month in full. You keep every credit you earned.

4. Monthly membership

From month two, your membership renews monthly. Cancel anytime before your renewal date and you won't be charged again. We don't refund partial months, but you keep access until the end of the month you've paid for, unless the law where you live says otherwise.

5. Mistakes

If you're charged twice or by mistake, email us and we'll refund the extra charge in full.

6. Before you dispute a charge

If something doesn't look right, please email us first. We can usually fix it the same day, and it's faster for you than a bank dispute.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes won't take away a refund you were already entitled to when you paid.
